About Me

I'm a Business Application Developer with 4+ years of experience designing, building, and maintaining software for payroll, HRMS, CRM, inventory, billing, and business-workflow automation. My core toolkit is VB.NET WinForms and SQL Server — from database design and business-logic implementation through debugging, reporting, and production support.

I'm also expanding into full-stack web development with Next.js, Supabase, and Prisma. My flagship project here is an Advocate CRM — a client/case management application for a law firm with appointments, billing, hearings, and role-based authentication — built after migrating an earlier Google Sheets and Apps Script prototype to a proper full-stack architecture.

My domain background is in payroll and HRMS software, including HRMThread by Sensys Technologies, alongside implementation and production support for EasyPay, AssetExpert, EasyTDS, and ChequeMate. That gives my development work practical business-process depth, not just code — I understand what the numbers on a payslip actually need to mean.

For reporting, I build with Crystal Reports and RDLC for payslips, attendance, and management dashboards, and I automate day-to-day operations using Google Sheets, Google Forms, and Google Apps Script, along with advanced Excel (VBA and reporting automation). I also hold a Google Data Analytics Certificate, which sharpens how I work with data-driven systems and reporting.

Outside of technology, I'm a science and football enthusiast, a Bengali creative writer, and someone who genuinely enjoys cooking, problem-solving, and coding — usually in that order on a Sunday.
